AURELIA is a bespoke contemporary villa residence spanning approximately 900 m² of built living space over two levels, situated on a secluded 4,000 m² private Mediterranean parcel. It features 5 en-suite bedroom suites, 6 bathrooms, horizontal Roman travertine overhangs, and continuous floor-to-ceiling glass walls.

900 M² BUILT 4,000 M² PARCEL 5 EN-SUITE SUITES

The villa embodies contemporary organic modernism. The exterior shell is hand-clad in honed Roman travertine stone anchored to solid limestone bedrock, paired with bronze-black thermal glazing profiles. Interiors unite warm quarter-sawn oak millwork with monolithic travertine fireplaces and sculptural Calacatta marble culinary islands.

ROMAN TRAVERTINE CALACATTA MARBLE QUARTER-SAWN OAK

Outdoor amenities include a zero-edge heated infinity pool mirroring the Mediterranean horizon, an intimate sunken conversation lounge with fire feature, a covered alfresco dining pavilion, manicured grounds with centuries-old olive trees, and sun-bleached travertine lounging decks.

ZERO-EDGE INFINITY POOL SUNKEN FIRE LOUNGE ALFRESCO PAVILION

The estate features a private gated motor court with secure multi-vehicle covered parking, integrated biometric and smart access automation, discreet perimeter infrared security infrastructure, and dedicated high-clearance access routes ensuring total seclusion.

GATED MOTOR COURT SMART AUTOMATION TOTAL SECLUSION
